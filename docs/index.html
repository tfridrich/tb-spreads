  • FCR is paid for capacity only; it has no separate energy price.
  • FCR and aFRR capacity are auctioned daily in six 4-hour blocks. For Germany the price of the four German control areas is used (they are normally identical; where they differ, the highest).

All data from the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform: the SDAC day-ahead auction for the AT and DE-LU bidding zones, and the balancing data of the Austrian and German grid operators. Delivery days follow Central European time; daylight-saving days have 23 or 25 hours.
